# Dot Matrix Watch Face - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

Dot Matrix Watch Face ("the watch face") does not collect, store, share or sell
any personal data.

- The watch face contains no code of its own. It is a set of display resources
  rendered by the Wear OS watch face system.
- It requests no permissions and has no internet access.
- Complications you choose to show, such as heart rate, steps, battery or
  weather, are supplied by other apps on your watch and drawn by the system. The
  watch face does not receive, keep or transmit that data. How those apps handle
  your data is covered by their own privacy policies.
- Your customisation choices (accent colour, minute ring, complications) are
  stored on your watch by Wear OS.
